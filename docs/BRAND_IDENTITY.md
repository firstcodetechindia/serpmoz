# SERPMOZ brand identity

The logo system, how it is built and how to use it. All files are in `public/brand/` and are generated by `scripts/build-brand.py`, so the symbol and the lettering can be adjusted in one place.

## Concept

**Search, discovery, direction, growth.**

- **The ring** is the search field: the place a customer looks, and the loop of continuous optimisation. It is not a magnifying glass; it has no handle and no lens highlight.
- **The arrow** leaves the centre of the ring at 45 degrees and passes through it. It stands for direction and business outcomes: visibility that leads somewhere.
- **The cut.** Where the arrow crosses the ring, the ring is cut away. The two shapes read as one drawn form and the cut is what keeps the symbol recognisable at 16 pixels.
- **The crescent.** A blue-to-cyan crescent sits on the lower left of the ring, the AI layer working underneath. Cyan is a supporting accent and never dominates.

The symbol is constructed from first principles on a 64-unit grid. It is not taken from an icon library.

| Element | Geometry (64 grid) |
| --- | --- |
| Ring | centre 27, 37; mid radius 16; thickness 10 |
| Arrow shaft | from 29, 35 to 50.5, 13.5; thickness 7.5; round ends |
| Arrow head | right-angled triangle 38.5, 5.5 to 58.5, 5.5 to 58.5, 25.5 |
| Cut | a 13-unit band along the arrow's line |
| Crescent | the ring minus a circle of radius 18.6 centred at 31.2, 32.8 |

## Typography

- **Wordmark:** Montserrat Bold, capitals, tracked slightly open. "SERP" in navy, "MOZ" in blue. The letters are stored as outlines, so no logo file needs the font installed. Montserrat is under the SIL Open Font License (`scripts/brand/Montserrat-LICENSE.txt`).
- **Tagline:** "AI-POWERED DIGITAL GROWTH" in Montserrat SemiBold, letterspaced so it is exactly as wide as the name.
- **Website text** stays in Geist. Montserrat is used for the logo only.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep a margin equal to the ring's thickness (10 units on the 64 grid, about one sixth of the symbol's height) free on every side.
- **Symbol alone:** 16 px minimum.
- **Symbol and name:** 20 px tall minimum (about 90 px wide).
- **With tagline:** 40 px tall minimum. Below that, drop the tagline.

## Backgrounds

- **Light grounds** (white, `#F7F9FC`): the primary colour logo.
- **Navy and dark grounds:** `logo-white`. The ring and "SERP" turn white, "MOZ" stays blue, the arrow stays orange.
- **Photographs and busy grounds:** place the logo on a navy or white panel, or use a one-colour version.
- **Blue grounds:** one-colour white only.

## Favicon and app icons

The tab icon is the symbol only, white ring on navy, with tighter padding so it holds at 16 px. The name is never placed inside an icon.

Wired in the site as:

- `app/icon.svg` and `app/favicon.ico` for browser tabs
- `app/apple-icon.png` for iOS home screens
- `app/manifest.ts` lists `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png`
- Organisation schema uses `logo-icon.png`

## Website header

The header, footer and mobile menu use the `Logo` component in `components/navigation/logo.tsx` with the tagline under the name (`<Logo tagline />`). For this size the tagline is set larger and closer than in the print lockups, still exactly as wide as the name, so it stays readable: the name's capitals are about 22 px and the tagline's about 6.5 px on desktop. It is drawn inline so the ring, "SERP" and the tagline follow the header's colour: navy on the white header, white over a dark hero.

In the header the logo sits on the left with clear space around it, the menu is centred in the bar and the actions are on the right. The header menu holds Solutions, Industries, Case Studies and Company; Locations and Resources are in the footer.

`<Logo />` without the tagline is the compact form for tight spaces, and `LogoMark` is the symbol on its own.

## Do not

- Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows, glows or 3D effects.
- Change the colours, or set "SERP" and "MOZ" in the same colour except in the one-colour versions.
- Recolour the arrow anything but orange (or the single colour in a one-colour version).
- Rebuild the name in another typeface, or in light weights.
- Put the full name inside a favicon or app icon.
- Place the colour logo on a mid-tone or clashing ground.
- Use the print tagline lockup below 40 px tall; use the header form of the tagline instead.
- Combine the symbol with other icons (robots, brains, rockets, magnifying glasses).

## Regenerating the files

```
python3 scripts/build-brand.py
```

writes every SVG and `components/navigation/logo-paths.ts`. The PNG and ICO files are rasterised from those SVGs (they were exported with headless Chromium at 16, 32, 180, 192, 512, 1024 and 1600 px wide); re-export them if the geometry changes.

## Open point

The name contains "MOZ", which is an existing brand in the search-software category. Take trademark advice before investing heavily in this identity.
