# Content style guide

For every location, service and article page.

## Voice
- Plain, professional British English. Short sentences. No em or en dashes.
- Say what the work is and what it involves. Prefer a concrete noun to an adjective.
- Banned: genuinely, honestly, straightforward, leverage, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, robust, delve, landscape, navigate, elevate, "leading", "best", "#1".

## Honesty
- No statistics, prices, client names, awards, or timelines presented as fact.
- Never describe how people in a place search, choose, read reviews, travel or prefer. SERPMOZ has no local data.
- Never describe a market as growing, competitive, crowded, digital-first or booming.
- Never say "most businesses here" or "increasingly".
- Never imply an office or team: no "our office", "our team in", "local team". Say once that the work is remote.
- State what cannot be promised: rankings, traffic, leads, citation in AI Overviews, ChatGPT or Gemini.
- Regulation: keep it general and say that the client should take advice.

## Allowed forms
- Common-knowledge facts: names, administration, language, time zone, sectors a place is known for ("is known for").
- Conditionals about the client's business: "If most of your customers come from a few neighbourhoods, the profile should name them."
- What SERPMOZ does and checks.

## Structure
- One H1. H2s are sections. Headings are statements or questions, not keyword strings.
- Answer first, 40 to 80 words.
- No lists of localities for keywords. A locality appears only where it helps set up a listing correctly.
- Descriptive link text ("AI SEO in India"), never "click here".
- No sentence is reused across pages. Pages are not produced by swapping the place name.
